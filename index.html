Command Centre

A morning brief for one person.

Command Centre runs on its owner's Mac. Each morning it reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar and builds a one-page summary of the day: mail that needs a reply, payments and deadlines, and the calendar.

One person uses it. There is no sign-up and nothing to download.

Google access

The tool asks for two permissions:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly)
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly)

With these it cannot send, delete or change mail, and it cannot create or edit events.
